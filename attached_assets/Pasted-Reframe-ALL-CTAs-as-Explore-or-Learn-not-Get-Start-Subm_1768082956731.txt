Reframe ALL CTAs as "Explore" or "Learn," not "Get/Start/Submit"
Current	Neuro-Aligned	Why It Works
"Start Safety Protocol"	"Explore the Safety Protocol"	"Explore" = intellectual curiosity, not service enrollment
"Submit Your Plot for Audit"	"Run a quick self-check on any plot"	"Run yourself" = you're in control, we're just showing you the method
"Get Your Decision Report"	"Build your own decision report"	"Build" = active participation, not receiving a service
"Chat with Expert"	"Discuss your specific plot" or just remove it	Removes transactional feel; if you keep it, make it optional/supplementary
Neuroscience behind this: The brain interprets active verbs (explore, build, check, run) as intellectual agency. Passive verbs (get, submit, receive) trigger consumer mode.
