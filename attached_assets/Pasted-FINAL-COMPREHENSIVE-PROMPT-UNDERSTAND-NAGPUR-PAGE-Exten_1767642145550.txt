Solution: Make Each Red Flag Contextual & Educational

EXAMPLE TRANSFORMATIONS:
For Capital Appreciation Investors (Short-Term):

❌ CURRENT: "Avoid TP scheme disputes"
✅ NEW INSIGHT:
"🚨 TP Scheme Risk for Short-Term Gains
Why it matters: TP scheme disputes delay land transfers by 6-18 months, killing quick exit opportunities. Property locked = no profit timing.
Impact on YOUR goal: You miss market window. Price momentum halts. Quick gains become slow gains.
How to verify: Get TP scheme clearance certificate from municipality. Ask lawyer: 'Is this property in any TP scheme?' Must be written confirmation."

For Home to Live In Buyers (Immediate):

❌ CURRENT: "Avoid regulatory delays"
✅ NEW INSIGHT:
"🚨 Regulatory Delay Risk for Your Home
Why it matters: Licensing delays mean possession delays. You want to move-in but can't. Building approvals missing = unfinished construction, stress.
Impact on YOUR life: Delayed move-in date. Kids change schools. Lease commitment extends. Frustration.
How to verify: Ask builder: 'Show me all municipal approvals.' Check possession timeline in agreement. Meet 5 residents - did they get possession on time?
Green flag: Builder has clear approval documents, past projects delivered on time."

For Rental Income Seekers:

❌ CURRENT: "Check for litigation"
✅ NEW INSIGHT:
"🚨 Litigation Risk for Your Rental Income
Why it matters: Disputes in area = tenant issues likely. Disputed properties = hard to rent. Tenant problems = missed rent, legal costs, vacant periods.
Impact on YOUR income: Months with 0 rent. Legal fees pile up. Property sits empty. Your 5% yield becomes 0%.
How to verify: Contact 10 residents asking: 'Any neighbor disputes?' Check court records for area. Meet existing tenants to understand satisfaction.
Green flag: Clean title, no disputes, residents satisfied, easy tenant turnover."

For First-Time Buyers:

❌ CURRENT: "Check for litigation"
✅ NEW INSIGHT:
"🚨 Litigation Risk - First-Timer Must Know
Why it matters: As a first-timer, complex disputes drain your money & time. Litigation areas = expensive lawyers, long court cases, property locked.
Impact on YOU: You'll regret purchase. Stress. Lost money in legal fees. Can't sell easily.
How to verify: Hire a property lawyer (costs ₹5-10K but saves ₹5-50L). They'll check: Title disputes, TP issues, municipal violations, neighbor conflicts.
Safe choice: Areas with clean title, zero litigation, verified municipal records."

For Business Owners:

❌ CURRENT: "Avoid TP scheme disputes"
✅ NEW INSIGHT:
"🚨 TP Scheme Risk for Your Business
Why it matters: TP scheme = planned construction changes. Your shop's main road might be diverted. Customer entry blocked during construction. Utilities relocated. Business halts.
Impact on YOUR business: Customer traffic stops for months. Revenue plummets. You still pay rent. Competition takes customers. Business fails.
How to verify: Visit municipal planning office. Ask: 'What's planned for this road next 5 years?' Check master plan. Talk to 20 shopkeepers - any planned disruptions?
Safe choice: Established commercial areas with no planned changes, or areas where changes already completed."
