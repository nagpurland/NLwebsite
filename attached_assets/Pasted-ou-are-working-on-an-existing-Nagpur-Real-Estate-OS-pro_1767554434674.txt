ou are working on an existing Nagpur Real Estate OS project in Replit.

Objective: Create individual property detail pages at the route /property/<id> that show full property context, micro-market data, legal status, and empowerment messaging so buyers can make informed decisions.

Current problem: When users browse properties in the /find-properties search, they see property cards with minimal information. Clicking a property should show a detailed page with market context, legal flags, and a clear explanation of "why this property matters for you."

WHAT EACH PROPERTY DETAIL PAGE SHOULD SHOW
1. Property Header Section
Property image (or placeholder if none available)

Locality and micro-location (e.g., "MIHAN, Nagpur")

Property type (Plot / Flat / Villa / Commercial)

Area/size (e.g., "1200 sqft carpet area")

Price: total price AND price per sqft

Date listed and last updated

2. "Why We Flagged This" Section (CRITICAL)
This is the EMPOWERMENT section—explain WHY this property was shown to the user.

Examples:

"✅ Undervalued by 18% vs area average (₹3,500 psf vs ₹4,200 average)"

"✅ Matches your 5% yield target: ₹50,000 annual rent on ₹10L investment"

"✅ Safe locality, no legal disputes found in last 5 years"

"✅ High growth area: prices up 15% YoY, Metro project incoming"

"✅ Near upcoming MIHAN expansion, strong demand pressure"

Show 2–3 key reasons why this property is good based on how it matches the user's profile or market conditions.

3. Micro-Market Context Section
Show how this property compares to the area:

"Area average psf: ₹4,200 | This property: ₹3,500 psf (17% below avg)"

"Average rental yield in this locality: 4.2% | This property: 5.1% (strong)"

"Recent price trend: +15.2% in 12 months (investor interest)"

"Active properties in area: 12 plots, 8 flats, 3 villas"

"Days on market: 23 days (average for area: 45 days — this is hot)"

This helps buyers see if this is a good deal relative to surroundings.

4. Legal Status Widget (CRITICAL)
Show legal safety clearly:

Option A (Safe):

text
✅ LEGAL STATUS: No major legal flags found
- NA (Non-Agricultural) status: ✅ Verified
- Recent disputes: ✅ None found
- TP scheme: ✅ Clear
- Title history: ✅ Clean (5+ years)
Option B (Needs Verification):

text
⚠️ LEGAL STATUS: Manual verification recommended
- NA status: ⚠️ Check with local NMC
- Recent disputes: ⚠️ 1 case (resolved 2023)
- TP scheme: ⚠️ Part of scheme, land acquisition phase
- Title history: ✅ Clean
Include a small note: "We flag obvious risks based on public records. Always verify independently or consult a lawyer."

5. Infra & Development Timeline
Show what's coming to the area:

Example:

text
🚧 UPCOMING INFRA (Reasons for appreciation)
- Metro Phase 2: Expected 2027 (2 km away, 25% complete)
- Ring Road connection: Under construction (1.5 km, 60% complete)
- New IT Park: Announced for 2025 (3 km away)
- School expansion: 2026 (1 km away)
This helps buyers understand why prices may rise.

6. Rental Market Data (If applicable)
For investors:

text
💰 RENTAL POTENTIAL
- Average monthly rent for similar 1BHK: ₹15,000–18,000
- Annual gross yield: 5.1% (₹50k rent ÷ ₹10L price)
- Tenant demand: High (IT professionals, expats)
- Occupancy rate in area: 92% (strong)
7. Source & Transparency
Be clear about where the data comes from:

text
📍 SOURCE & VERIFICATION
- Listed on: [Portal Name]
- Date listed: March 15, 2025
- Last updated: Today
- View original listing: [LINK to source URL]

All price and property data verified from public sources.
Availability and terms subject to change — verify with seller/agent.
8. CTAs (Calls to Action)
"Schedule a Site Visit" → form to book a time

"Ask an Expert" → WhatsApp pre-filled with property details

"Save for Later" → add to shortlist (if user logged in)

"Share on WhatsApp" → pre-filled message with property details + link

"Ask Legal Question" → deep-dive legal review option

ROUTE & DATA FLOW
Route: /property/<id>

Example: /property/mihan-plot-2024-001

Get property data from properties table using the ID

Fetch related data from:

properties → main property details

daily_reports → locality pricing trends

infra_events → upcoming projects (filtered by locality + distance)

rules_and_regulations → legal flags for this locality

Dispute records (if exist) → legal history for this specific property/area

Property rental history (if exists) → rental yield data
