# Legal Readiness Gate

NagpurLand does not accept real property-review cases until every item below is
closed. An advocate being available does not close this gate.

**Status: CLOSED to cases.** Inquiries only.

Disabled until the gate is approved: document uploads, legal-service payments and
automated legal opinions.

Drafted by the project team for the advocate's review. This is not legal advice.

## Service boundaries (draft for the advocate)

| Service | What it is | Who provides it | What it is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Educational property screening | Guides and a self-completed checklist | NagpurLand | Legal advice, or proof of clear title or safety |
| Administrative document assistance | Explaining which documents a buyer may need and organizing the handoff, once condition 7 is closed | NagpurLand | Examining or giving an opinion on a document |
| Professional legal due diligence | Title review and legal opinion | Independent advocate engaged by the buyer | A NagpurLand service or a NagpurLand conclusion |

NagpurLand must never imply that the 12-point checklist independently establishes
clear title.
